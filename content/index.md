# Hi, I'm Marcos Horno

This is my digital garden—a collection of notes on software engineering, technology, science, cooking, philosophy, finances, and whatever else catches my curiosity. Think of it as a public learning journal where ideas grow and connect over time.

## What You'll Find Here

This digital garden explores various aspects of software engineering:

- **[[Software]]** - Core software engineering concepts including data structures, algorithms, system architecture, and more
- **System Design** - Architecture patterns, distributed systems, scalability
- **Data & Storage** - Databases, caching, data structures
- **Networking & APIs** - Communication protocols, REST, GraphQL, messaging
- **DevOps & Operations** - Docker, Kubernetes, monitoring, CI/CD

## About Digital Gardens

Unlike traditional blogs with chronological posts, this is a digital garden where notes are continuously cultivated, updated, and interconnected. Ideas are linked together, forming a network of knowledge rather than a linear timeline.

Feel free to explore! Use the graph view to discover connections between topics, or browse through the explorer to navigate by topic.
